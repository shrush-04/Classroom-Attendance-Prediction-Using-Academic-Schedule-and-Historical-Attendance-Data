### **Logbook Entry Guidelines:**
1. **Source verification:** Only use the actual classroom register, department attendance sheet, or laboratory logbook to fill in "Students Present". Do not guess or record estimated numbers.
2. **Timetable change:** Note if this lecture was rescheduled or swapped.
3. **Privacy protection:** Never write down individual student names, roll numbers, or personal remarks (such as "STU001 was sick"). Keep comments strictly aggregate (e.g., "Heavy rain", "Swapped with Cloud Computing").
4. **Initials:** The recorder must sign or write their initials to verify data accuracy.
